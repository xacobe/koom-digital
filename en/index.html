Our story

Why Koom?

In Mooré, koom means water — what nourishes, what gives life, what connects communities. It is also the symbol of Drupal, the CMS we use to build our most ambitious projects.

A coincidence? Maybe. But to us, it's a coherence: an agency rooted in its land, built with the world's best tools, in service of the digital growth of Burkina Faso and the wider Sahel region.
