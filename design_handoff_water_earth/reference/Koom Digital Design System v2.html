00 — Foundations

An agency rooted in its soil, built with the world's best tools.

In Mooré, koom means water — what nourishes, gives life, connects communities. It is also the emblem of Drupal, the CMS we build on. v2 keeps that water at the core and adds the red earth of Burkina Faso — laterite ochre — so the identity now holds both halves of the promise: local roots, global reach.

What changed in v2

  • The logo loses the diamond — the drop is now woven from water and earth
  • Ochre becomes a true secondary colour, not a footnote
  • Faso Dan Fani, bògòlanfini & banco motifs are more present, threaded through every seam
  • Still calm, credible and accessible — built for funders and ministries

Voice

“Local presence, international standards.”

Confident, plain-spoken, never corporate. We address a market trader in Ouaga and a UN funder in the same calm, credible register — across four languages.

01 — Logo & mark

The drop, rewoven.

Out goes the diamond behind the mark. The new logomark is a single drop of water whose body is woven like Faso Dan Fani cloth — blue water above, ochre earth below. One glyph now carries water (koom), the Drupal drop, hand-woven craft, and the laterite ground of Burkina Faso.

The mark system — three forms, one idea

Woven drop Primary

The signature mark — water above, earth below, a single highlight ripple. Use wherever space allows.

Badge

Drop on a navy tile with an ochre earth-line. For favicons, app icons and avatars.

Monoline

Single-weight outline with one ochre stroke. For stamps, embroidery, one-colour print.

Primary lockup

Koom Digital

On light — the woven drop beside the wordmark.

Koom Digital

On navy — the drop keeps its woven body; the wordmark reverses to white + light blue.

Construction & clear space

  • Drop drawn on a 64-unit grid — height ≈ 42, width ≈ 26
  • Woven body: water band fills the top ~60%
  • Earth line — sand → ochre → deep-ochre — sits at the waterline
  • One white ripple, top-left, at 55% opacity
Koom Digital

Keep clear space ≥ the height of the drop on every side.

Do & don't

Keep water above, earth below

Don't desaturate the weave

Don't stretch or distort

Don't sit it on ochre

04 — Cultural motifs

The thread of Burkina Faso, woven through.

In v2 the West African references step forward. They are structural, not ornamental: a bolder bògòlanfini lattice, a warm woven seam stitching every section, a mudcloth dentil band, and the banco frieze on dark edges. Now carrying ochre, they read unmistakably as Burkinabè.

The weave seam · Faso Dan Fani, now with earth

The signature element. A multi-stripe woven band echoing locally-spun Faso Dan Fani cotton — a Burkinabè symbol of self-reliance — now threading ochre and sand through the blues. It divides every section of this document edge to edge.

Bogolan · dark

Bògòlanfini lattice. Interlocking diamonds in ochre over color fields — Malian–Burkinabè mud-cloth.

Bogolan · light

The same lattice in blue on washed surfaces and heroes.

Mudcloth band

Dentil mudcloth. A zig-zag earth band for footers, card edges and the cover base.

Banco frieze

Sahel frieze. Triangular dentils echoing banco-mosque parapets (Bobo-Dioulasso, Djenné).

Mudcloth accent band

--mudcloth — a repeating SVG dentil in --koom-ochre on navy. Use as a base rule on dark sections, business cards and the footer.

05 — Spacing & layout

One rhythm, top to bottom.

06 — Iconography

Line icons in a faceted well.
